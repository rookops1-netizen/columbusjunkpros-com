Hot Tub Removal in Columbus, OH

Old hot tubs and spas are notoriously difficult to remove. They're heavy, awkward, and often require disassembly to fit through doorways and gates. At Columbus Junk Pros, we specialize in hot tub removal throughout Columbus and Central Ohio. Our experienced team has the equipment and expertise to remove hot tubs of all sizes and types — from small two-person spas to large eight-person models, including in-ground and above-ground units. We handle everything from draining and disconnecting to hauling and disposal.

Our Hot Tub Removal Process

Hot tub removal requires careful planning and execution. We start by assessing access points — measuring doorways, gates, and pathways to determine the best removal route. We drain the hot tub and disconnect any electrical or plumbing connections. If necessary, we disassemble the hot tub into manageable sections. We use specialized equipment including dollies, straps, and ramps to move the hot tub safely without damaging your property. We load it onto our truck and haul it away for proper disposal. We recycle the components — the shell, pumps, heaters, and wiring are all recyclable materials.

Types of Hot Tubs We Remove

We remove all types of hot tubs and spas. This includes above-ground portable spas, in-ground hot tubs, swim spas, inflatable hot tubs, acrylic shell spas, and rotomolded spas. We also remove related equipment including hot tub covers, steps, and accessories. Whether your hot tub is in the backyard, on a deck, in a sunroom, or in a basement, we can get it out. We've removed hot tubs from some of the most challenging locations in Columbus — tight backyards, narrow gates, and steep hillsides.

Hot Tub Removal Challenges

Hot tub removal presents unique challenges that require professional expertise. Hot tubs are extremely heavy — even empty, a standard 6-person hot tub weighs 500-800 pounds, and larger models can exceed 1,000 pounds. They're awkwardly shaped, making them difficult to maneuver through doorways, gates, and around corners. They often require disassembly to fit through tight spaces. They may be located in difficult-to-access areas like backyards with narrow gates, decks, or basements. They contain electrical and plumbing connections that need to be properly disconnected. And they require proper disposal — the shells are not biodegradable and cannot be disposed of in regular trash. Professional removal addresses all these challenges safely and efficiently.

What Happens to Your Old Hot Tub

When we remove your old hot tub, we ensure it's disposed of responsibly. The acrylic shell is recycled when possible — it can be ground up and used in new products. The pumps, heaters, and control systems contain metals and electronic components that are recycled. The wiring and plumbing are separated for recycling. Any remaining water is drained and disposed of properly. We're committed to keeping as much as possible out of landfills.
