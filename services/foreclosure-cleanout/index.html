Foreclosure Cleanout Services in Columbus

Foreclosure properties require thorough, efficient cleanout services to prepare them for resale or rental. At Columbus Junk Pros, we specialize in foreclosure and eviction cleanout services throughout Columbus and Central Ohio. What We Handle in Foreclosure Cleanouts

Foreclosure cleanouts often involve removing all contents from a property. This includes furniture, appliances, clothing, personal belongings, food from kitchens, yard equipment, vehicles (if on the property), and general household debris. We also handle more challenging situations including hoarder cleanouts, properties with pest infestations, and properties with significant damage from neglect or vandalism. Our team is experienced, professional, and non-judgmental — we handle every situation with discretion and respect.

Our Foreclosure Cleanout Process

We start with a site assessment to understand the scope of work and identify any special requirements. We provide a detailed quote and timeline. Our crew arrives with the right equipment and manpower to clear the property efficiently. We sort items for donation (usable items go to local Columbus charities), recycling, and disposal. We clean the space after removal, leaving it broom-clean and ready for showings or renovation. We provide documentation of disposal for your records. Most properties are cleared in 1-3 days.

Hoarder Cleanout Services

Hoarder situations require special handling. These properties often contain large volumes of items that need to be removed, and the work can be emotionally challenging for all involved. Our team is trained in hoarder cleanout protocols. We approach every situation with compassion and non-judgmental professionalism. We work with family members, social workers, and mental health professionals when needed. We can coordinate with cleaning services for post-removal deep cleaning. We handle biohazard situations (animal waste, mold, etc.) with proper safety protocols. We understand the sensitivity of these situations and handle them with the care and discretion they deserve.
