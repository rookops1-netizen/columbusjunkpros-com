Why Choose Professional Appliance Removal?

Old appliances are heavy, awkward, and often contain hazardous materials. Refrigerators and freezers contain refrigerants that must be recovered by certified technicians — it's illegal to dispose of them in landfills without proper refrigerant recovery. Washing machines and dryers are heavy and difficult to move without the right equipment. Professional removal ensures safety, proper disposal, and convenience. Plus, we recycle the metal components, keeping waste out of Columbus landfills.

Environmental Impact of Appliance Disposal

Proper appliance disposal is important for environmental protection. Old refrigerators and freezers contain refrigerants that are potent greenhouse gases — if released into the atmosphere, they contribute to climate change. These refrigerants must be recovered by EPA-certified technicians using specialized equipment. Appliances also contain metals, plastics, and other materials that can be recycled. A typical refrigerator contains 100-200 pounds of recyclable steel. By choosing professional appliance removal, you ensure that your old appliances are disposed of responsibly, with refrigerants properly recovered and materials recycled whenever possible. It's the right choice for your home and for the environment.
